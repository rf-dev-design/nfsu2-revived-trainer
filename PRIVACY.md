# Privacy Policy

**U2 Revived Trainer**
Last updated: 7 November 2026

U2 Revived Trainer is an unofficial, fan-made browser extension for the Underground 2 Revived game at nfsu2.xyz. This policy covers the extension only.

## Data we collect

None. The extension doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share any user data, and it has no analytics or tracking. It makes no network requests, so nothing it handles leaves your device.

## What stays on your device

Panel settings, like the boost multiplier, are saved in the page's local storage for nfsu2.xyz on your device, so they're still there next time you play. They're never sent anywhere by the extension. You can remove them by clearing your browser's site data for nfsu2.xyz.

## Site access

The extension runs only on `https://nfsu2.xyz/*` and requests no other permissions. It reads and changes the game's memory in that page to provide the trainer's features.

## Third parties

The extension shares nothing with third parties. nfsu2.xyz is a separate site that the extension's developer doesn't run, so this policy doesn't cover what that site does.

## Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published in this file, with a new date above.
